Grow something
that outlives you.

Every forest begins with a single, quiet decision. Watch it grow.

Chapter I · Reaching

First, it learns
the shape of the sky.

For the first decade a tree spends almost everything on structure — a trunk to stand on and a few bold limbs to find the light.

Chapter II · Branching

Then, it reaches
for everything at once.

Limbs divide, and divide again. Each new twig is a small bet on tomorrow’s sun, and together they begin to cast real shade.

Chapter III · Canopy

Finally,
it becomes a place.

Shelter for birds, shade for strangers, a landmark for people not yet born. This is what you’re really planting.
